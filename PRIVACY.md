# Privacy Policy — Tab Switch

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

Tab Switch does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What it reads:** the titles, addresses and icons of your open tabs — only to show them in the switcher list
  at the moment you open it.
- **Where it goes:** nowhere. Everything stays inside your browser. The extension makes no network requests
  and has no analytics.
- **What it stores:** the list currently shown (in session memory, cleared when the browser closes) and an internal
  version marker used during development. No browsing history is saved.
- **Page access:** the switcher is drawn on top of the current page. The extension does not read or change page
  content.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/BogdanFlatANDHome/tabswitch/issues
